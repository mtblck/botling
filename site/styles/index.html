Styles

Each style is its own family, with its own entry point, palettes and parts. A seed grows a different seedkin in each, and the same one every time.

    • Botling Stable

      Robots with screens, antennas, visors and grilles, in 16 palettes and four cell shapes. seedkin/botling

      • Snoot Preview

        Animals: cats, foxes, bears, bunnies, mice, dogs, pandas, owls, frogs and pigs, in eight coats. seedkin/snoot

        • Noggin Coming soon

          People, with skin tones, hair, glasses, beards and hats.

          • Beastie Preview

            Monsters: blobs, ghosts and boxes with one to three eyes, fangs, horns and wings, in eight hides. seedkin/beastie

          A Preview style is in the package, but until it's marked Stable the avatar a seed draws can change in a minor version. Coming soon means it's still in the lab.
