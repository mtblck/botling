Meet the Sprites.

Wherever the Grid ran hot, wet, stony, breezy or starry, a Sprite came up: a flame with a swaying tip, a wobbly drop, a pebble with a sprout, a gust trailing curls, a ringed planet. Each one has a face. Give one some text and it draws the same Sprite every time, on any machine, with nothing stored. Type anything to meet yours.
