Seedkin Render Looks

The same 15×15 cells, drawn six ways. Gapped is how every family draws today. The other five close the gap so the cells merge into flat shapes, then try outlines, rounded corners and a sticker border on top. Each would be an option next to today's look, not a replacement.

The looks

Gapped

Today's renderer: separate cells over a faint grid. Reads as pixel art on a display.

Flat

No gap and no grid, so cells merge into solid colour shapes. Works with every cell shape: circles become beads, diamonds and triangles tile into facets. The small 9×9 avatars already draw this way.

Inked

Flat plus a dark outline round the silhouette and thin lines between colours, like a cel-shaded cartoon. Inner lines on square cells only.

Soft

Flat with every run of one colour rounded at its outer corners and filleted inside, so it reads as vector shapes. Square cells only; other shapes fall back to Flat.

Soft inked

Soft plus the silhouette outline. The most "illustrated" of the set.

Sticker

Soft with a thick white border and a drop shadow, like a die-cut sticker. Shrinks the picture a little to make room.
