Seedkin lab · concept, not in the package

Sprites

Little elemental spirits that flicker. A seed picks an element (fire, water, earth, wind or space), one of two forms, and a palette. Each sprite swaps between four frames and dims now and then, like a firefly, but never goes fully out.

Does it work?

  • Yes, with eyes. Abstract shapes alone drift toward animated identicons or weather icons, and lose what makes Seedkin a family of characters. With eyes they become will-o'-wisps and soot sprites, which sit happily next to the robots.
  • Fire and space are strong, water is good, earth is fine, wind is hard. Each element needs a silhouette people already know. Wind has none, so it relies on speed lines and motion.
  • Flicker belongs in the motes, not the body. Embers, bubbles, pebbles and stars blinking around a sprite give the firefly feel. A body that vanishes looks like a loading bug in a list of avatars. The still frame has to stand alone too, since most avatars are drawn still.
  • Too few combinations today. 5 elements × 2 forms × 3 palettes is 30 sprites, so many seeds share a look. It needs more traits before it could ship: eye styles, a mouth, mote patterns, sizes and more palettes.
  • Elements are a nice hook. Letting people pick an element (element: 'fire') is something no other style has, and it suits teams, games and status.
